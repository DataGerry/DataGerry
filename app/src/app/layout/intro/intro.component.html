Welcome to your first DataGerry launch.

The assistant can help you setup up your initial structure for DataGerry in a few simple steps.
Try it out by clicking the 'Start Assistant'-button.
Click the Cancel-button to start with an empty data model to define your own.

The Assistant can also be started by pressing " => Assistant" in the top right corner.


The assistant is only usable when no categories, types and objects exist in the database.
