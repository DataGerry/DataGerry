Catalogs

Reference catalogs in OSCAL format that ship with DATAGERRY. They are read-only and open in the bundled OSCAL viewer; no data leaves this server. Import creates the requirements of a catalog as controls; Update takes over changes of the catalog and Delete removes the imported controls again.
