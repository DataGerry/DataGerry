ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A - control numbers and titles (assets/oscal/catalogs/iso-27001-2022-annex-a.json)

This catalog lists only the numbers and titles of the 93 information security controls of ISO/IEC 27001:2022
Annex A (identical to the control list of ISO/IEC 27002:2022) to reference them in an ISMS.

The control text, its purpose and the implementation guidance are protected by copyright of ISO and IEC and
are NOT included. Obtain the standard from ISO (https://www.iso.org/standard/27001) or, in Germany, as
DIN EN ISO/IEC 27001 from DIN Media, and add the text from your licensed copy to the imported controls.
