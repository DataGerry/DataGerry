1. OSCAL Viewer (assets/oscal/viewer/)
   Source:  https://github.com/EasyDynamics/oscal-viewer
   License: MIT (see oscal-viewer-MIT.txt)
   Changes: built as a static bundle with relative base path and hash routing;
            Google Analytics, cookie banner and Google Fonts import removed;
            document load timeout raised from 10 s to 120 s; documents may only
            be loaded from the hosting origin (cross-origin fetches are rejected);
            reduced to a read-only catalog viewer (other OSCAL models, upload,
            oscal.io registry/token UI removed) and restyled for DATAGERRY (colours,
            Raleway font, DATAGERRY logo and favicon, embedded mode). Upstream commit
